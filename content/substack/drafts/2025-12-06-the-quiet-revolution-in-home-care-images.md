These prompts are designed for nano banana or similar AI image generators. The style should match the Folk Care / Neighborhood Lab visual identity: warm terracotta and rust tones, muted sage green accents, cream/off-white backgrounds, simple line art illustration style with organic warmth, minimal and clean with soft rounded edges.

## Style Notes for All Images

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: Terracotta/rust (#C17A6F, #D98B7A)
- Secondary: Muted sage green (#7FA089)
- Background: Warm off-white/cream (#fdfcfb)
- Accent: Charcoal for line work (#2d3748)

**Visual Style:**
- Simple line art, not photorealistic
- Organic, hand-drawn feel with slight imperfections
- Rounded corners and soft edges
- Generous negative space
- No harsh shadows—soft, warm lighting implied
- Human-centered but often showing hands/gestures rather than faces
- Avoids clinical/medical aesthetic—feels like home, not hospital

**Mood:**
- Warm and approachable
- Dignified, not patronizing
- Community-oriented
- Hopeful but grounded
- Professional but human

**Technical:**
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 or similar horizontal format for Substack headers
- High contrast for readability at small sizes
- Works well in both light and dark modes
- Simple enough to load quickly
