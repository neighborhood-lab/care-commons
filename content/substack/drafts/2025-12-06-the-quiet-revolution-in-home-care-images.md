# Image Prompts for "The Quiet Revolution in Home Care"

These prompts are designed for AI image generators to match the warm, dignified visual style of Neighborhood Lab's existing Substack imagery. The style should be: soft digital illustration with warm muted tones, clean linework with gentle gradients, cozy domestic settings, human-centered without being overly sentimental.

**Color Palette Reference:**
- Warm muted taupes, beiges, cream backgrounds
- Muted teal/sage greens for accents
- Terracotta/rust accents
- Soft golden natural lighting
- Avoid harsh shadows or clinical aesthetics

## Technical Notes for All Images

**Style Consistency:**
- Soft digital illustration (not photorealistic, not overly cartoonish)
- Clean linework with subtle texture
- Gentle gradients, no harsh shadows
- Warm natural lighting (golden hour quality)
- Muted, sophisticated color palette

**Mood Consistency:**
- Dignified and warm (never patronizing or overly cute)
- Human-centered (people are comfortable, in control)
- Hopeful but grounded (realistic, not utopian)
- Domestic and cozy (home settings, not clinical/corporate)
- Professional but human (serious work, caring context)

**What to Avoid:**
- Clinical/medical aesthetics (no white coats, hospitals, medical equipment)
- Corporate coldness (no glass towers, boardrooms, suits)
- Overly sentimental imagery (no angel wings, excessive hearts, crying)
- Cartoon exaggeration (keep it gently realistic)
- Harsh lighting or dark shadows
- Busy, cluttered compositions

**Accessibility:**
- High contrast between subjects and backgrounds
- Works well at small sizes (mobile/email)
- Readable even if colors are muted by some displays
- No essential information conveyed by color alone
