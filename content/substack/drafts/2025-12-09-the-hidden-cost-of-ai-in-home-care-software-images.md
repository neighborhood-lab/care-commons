# Image Prompts for "The Hidden Cost of AI in Home Care Software"

These prompts are designed for AI image generators (Ideogram/nano banana style) to match the warm, dignified visual style of Neighborhood Lab's existing Substack imagery. The style should be: soft digital illustration with warm muted tones, clean linework with gentle gradients, cozy domestic settings, human-centered without being overly sentimental.

**Color Palette Reference:**
- Warm muted taupes, beiges, cream backgrounds
- Muted teal/sage greens for accents
- Terracotta/rust accents
- Soft golden natural lighting
- Avoid harsh shadows or clinical aesthetics

---

## Image 1: The Two Paths

**Placement:** Near the opening section about the AI cost problem, or the "Quality Versus Cost" section

**Prompt:**
Digital illustration in warm muted tones showing a fork in a path viewed from above. On the left path, a simple trail leads through a cozy neighborhood of small cream and terracotta houses with glowing windows—suggesting the free, community option. On the right path, the trail leads toward a sleek but cold glass building with blue-tinted lights—suggesting the expensive API option. In the middle, where the paths diverge, sits a small wooden signpost. The left path is bathed in warm golden light; the right path has cooler, more clinical lighting. A few simplified trees with sage green foliage frame the scene. Style: soft digital illustration with warm muted palette, clean linework, gentle gradients. The mood is contemplative—showing a genuine choice between accessibility and capability, without demonizing either option.

**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 horizontal

## Technical Notes for All Images

**Style Consistency:**
- Soft digital illustration (not photorealistic, not overly cartoonish)
- Clean linework with subtle texture
- Gentle gradients, no harsh shadows
- Warm natural lighting (golden hour quality)
- Muted, sophisticated color palette

**Mood Consistency:**
- Dignified and warm (never patronizing or overly cute)
- Human-centered (people are comfortable, in control)
- Hopeful but grounded (realistic, not utopian)
- Domestic and cozy (home settings, not clinical/corporate)
- Professional but human (serious work, caring context)

**What to Avoid:**
- Clinical/medical aesthetics (no white coats, hospitals, medical equipment)
- Corporate coldness (no glass towers, boardrooms, suits)
- Overly technical imagery (no circuit boards, server racks, code screens)
- Robot/AI imagery (no androids, glowing eyes, sci-fi elements)
- Harsh lighting or dark shadows
- Busy, cluttered compositions

**Accessibility:**
- High contrast between subjects and backgrounds
- Works well at small sizes (mobile/email)
- Readable even if colors are muted by some displays
- No essential information conveyed by color alone
