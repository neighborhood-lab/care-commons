# Care Commons - Demo Video Script (2-3 minutes)

**Target Audience:** Home healthcare agency administrators and coordinators  
**Goal:** Show how Care Commons solves operational pain points with state-specific compliance  
**Tone:** Professional, helpful, emphasizing ease of use

---

## Opening (0:00-0:15) - 15 seconds

**Visual:** Care Commons landing page

**Voiceover:**
> "Managing a home healthcare agency means juggling compliance, scheduling, EVV requirements, and billing—all while delivering quality care. Care Commons brings everything together in one modern platform that adapts to your state's specific regulations."

**On-screen text:**
- "Care Commons"
- "Shared care software, community owned"

---

## Dashboard Overview (0:15-0:45) - 30 seconds

**Visual:** Navigate to /dashboard showing coordinator view

**Voiceover:**
> "Your dashboard gives you real-time visibility. See pending tasks, upcoming visits, compliance alerts, and quick actions—all in one place. Whether you're a coordinator, supervisor, or administrator, the interface adapts to your role."

**Key UI elements to highlight:**
- Task list with priorities
- Visit calendar with status indicators
- Compliance alerts
- Quick action buttons

---

## Scheduling & EVV (0:45-1:30) - 45 seconds

**Visual:** Navigate to /scheduling then /evv and /mobile

**Voiceover:**
> "Scheduling is intuitive with visual calendars and conflict detection. Electronic Visit Verification meets your state's requirements automatically. Caregivers clock in from their mobile devices with GPS verification. The system validates the six required elements and generates compliant reports—whether you're in Texas, Florida, or any other state."

**Actions to show:**
- Create visit
- Assign caregiver  
- Mobile EVV clock-in
- GPS verification

---

## State-Specific Compliance (1:30-2:00) - 30 seconds

**Visual:** Navigate to /states

**Voiceover:**
> "Every state has different rules. Care Commons handles the complexity for you. From Texas's HHAeXchange requirements to Florida's background screening regulations, the system adapts automatically."

**Show:** Texas vs Florida comparison

---

## Closing (2:00-2:15) - 15 seconds

**Visual:** Landing page CTA

**Voiceover:**
> "Care Commons: open source, community-owned software. Try it free for 14 days—no credit card required."

**Call to Action:** Sign Up button
