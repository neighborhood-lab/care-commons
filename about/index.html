Folk Care

Shared care software, community owned

Complete Home Care Management Platform

Open-source software for care agencies, families, and communities. Built for transparency, compliance, and better care outcomes.

📅 Scheduling & Visits

Intelligent scheduling with caregiver matching, automated recurring visits, and real-time availability tracking.

✓ EVV Compliance

Built-in Electronic Visit Verification with GPS, mobile clock-in/out, and state-specific compliance (TX, FL, OH, CA, NY).

👥 Care Coordination

Centralized client profiles, care plans, medication tracking, and task management for coordinated care delivery.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Engagement

Family portal for updates, secure messaging, visit notes, and care plan access. Real-time transparency.

💰 Billing & Invoicing

Automated invoice generation from completed visits, insurance billing, payment tracking, and financial reporting.

📊 Reporting & Analytics

Compliance dashboards, caregiver performance metrics, client outcomes, and customizable reports.

Why Folk Care?

🆓 Open Source

🏥 Compliance First

Built for state regulations, Medicaid billing, and home care industry standards. EVV compliant out of the box.

🔒 Privacy & Security
