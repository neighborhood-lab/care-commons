## Executive Summary

**Axxess** is a major player in the home healthcare software market, serving 9,000+ organizations caring for 7M+ patients worldwide. Founded in 2007 in Dallas, Texas by John Olajide, Axxess provides a comprehensive cloud-based platform for home health, home care, hospice, and palliative care. With $37B+ in claims processed, 800,000+ users, and 200,000+ mobile app users, Axxess competes directly with WellSky for market leadership.

**Key Differentiators:**
- **Global scale:** 9,000+ organizations, 7M+ patients served, 800K+ users
- **AI-driven platform:** "Axxess intelligence" powers predictive analytics and automation
- **Comprehensive ecosystem:** Home health, home care, hospice, palliative care, RCM, staffing, training
- **Census-based pricing:** Tiered pricing by patient census (not per-user), unlimited users included
- **Cloud-native:** Browser-based, mobile apps (iOS/Android), distributed data centers
- **Industry leadership:** AGILE conference (9,000+ attendees), public policy advocacy, Care at Home Partner Award winner

**Competitive Positioning vs. Folk Care:**

Axxess is a **direct competitor** targeting the same home health/home care market. However, Axxess serves medium to large agencies with census-based pricing ($899+ reported, census tiers unclear), while Folk Care targets small agencies with flat-rate pricing ($30/month unlimited users). Folk Care's open-source model offers **disruptive cost advantage** ($1,080/3 years vs. estimated $32K-100K+ for Axxess).

## Strategic Insights

### Market Overlap

**Direct Competition:**
- Axxess and Folk Care both target **home health agencies** (Medicare-certified) and **home care agencies** (non-medical private duty)
- Both offer: scheduling, EVV, billing, care plans, caregiver management, family/patient engagement
- Both are cloud-based (Axxess cloud-only, Folk Care self-hosted or cloud)

**Market Segmentation:**
- **Axxess dominates:** Medium to large agencies (50+ patients, census-based pricing scales up)
- **Folk Care opportunity:** Small agencies (1-50 patients, flat-rate pricing scales down)

**Switching Barriers:**
- **Axxess → Folk Care:** High switching costs (data migration, retraining, loss of RCM/AI features), but massive cost savings ($32K-100K → $1K over 3 years estimated)
- **Folk Care → Axxess:** Unlikely (Folk Care users choose open-source for cost/flexibility, Axxess is opposite)

### Lessons for Folk Care

**High Priority (Directly Applicable):**

1. **Mobile App Completion (P0 - Critical):**
   - Axxess has 200,000+ mobile app users (launched 2014, 11-year head start)
   - Folk Care's mobile EVV app is work in progress
   - **Action:** Prioritize mobile app completion (table stakes for home care software)

2. **Census-Based Pricing Insight:**
   - Axxess uses census-based tiers (patient count) with unlimited users
   - Folk Care uses flat-rate ($30/month) with unlimited users
   - **Advantage:** Folk Care's flat-rate is simpler and more transparent
   - **Action:** Emphasize "no hidden fees, no census tiers, no setup fees" in marketing

3. **Claim Processing Automation (P1 - Medium Priority):**
   - Axxess processed $37B+ in claims (real-time automated submission)
   - Folk Care has basic invoicing
   - **Action:** Add automated Medicare/Medicaid claims submission (long-term roadmap, not immediate priority for small agencies)

4. **Patient Satisfaction Tracking (P2 - Low Priority):**
   - Axxess CAHPS tracks patient satisfaction in real-time (HHVBP quality metric)
   - Folk Care lacks patient engagement analytics
   - **Action:** Add basic patient satisfaction surveys (CAHPS integration for Medicare agencies)

5. **Training Platform (P2 - Low Priority):**
   - Axxess Certification+ offers on-demand compliance training
   - Folk Care lacks integrated training
   - **Action:** Add training module tracking (link to external LMS like Relias, HealthStream)

**Medium Priority (Transferable Concepts):**

6. **Staffing Marketplace (P3 - Low Priority):**
   - Axxess CARE connects agencies with clinicians (address caregiver shortages)
   - Folk Care lacks staffing solutions
   - **Action:** Consider marketplace for freelance caregivers (long-term, community-driven)

7. **Industry Leadership (P3 - Low Priority):**
   - Axxess AGILE conference draws 9,000+ attendees (thought leadership, networking)
   - Folk Care has smaller open-source community
   - **Action:** Host virtual Folk Care community events (open-source summits, webinars)

8. **Interoperability Partnerships (P1 - Medium Priority):**
   - Axxess has Kno2 API, Forcura, Optum, McKesson, CarePort integrations
   - Folk Care lacks HIE connections
   - **Action:** Add FHIR export, partner with HIE vendors (enables value-based care participation)

**Low Priority (Different Market Segment or Too Complex):**

9. **AI Predictive Analytics (P4 - Skip for Now):**
   - Axxess "intelligence" powered by data from 7M+ patients (network effects)
   - Folk Care can't compete on data scale without massive user base
   - **Action:** Focus on operational efficiency, skip AI until critical mass of users

10. **Enterprise Certifications (P4 - Skip for Now):**
    - Axxess has ACHC, HIGHTRUST, Soleir, CHAP certifications
    - Folk Care self-hosted shifts compliance responsibility to agency
    - **Action:** Document HIPAA best practices for self-hosted deployments (security checklist), skip formal certifications

### Threats to Folk Care

**Direct Threats:**

1. **Axxess Pricing Reduction for Small Agencies:**
   - If Axxess launches low-cost tier for agencies with <50 patients ($50-200/month), Folk Care's cost advantage shrinks
   - **Likelihood:** Low (census-based pricing assumes minimum census threshold, small agencies unprofitable)
   - **Mitigation:** Emphasize open-source advantages (data ownership, no vendor lock-in, community governance)

2. **Axxess Acquires Open-Source Competitor:**
   - Axxess could acquire a Folk Care-like open-source project (similar to WellSky's M&A strategy)
   - **Likelihood:** Low (open-source projects typically not acquisition targets, AGPL-3.0 prevents proprietary forks)
   - **Mitigation:** AGPL-3.0 license prevents proprietary forks (any Axxess fork must remain open-source)

3. **Axxess Adds QuickBooks Integration:**
   - If Axxess prioritizes QuickBooks integration, Folk Care loses potential differentiation
   - **Likelihood:** Medium (common customer request, Axxess has partnership ecosystem)
   - **Mitigation:** Build QuickBooks integration **now** before Axxess does

**Indirect Threats:**

4. **Mobile App Feature Gap:**
   - Axxess has 200K+ mobile users, 11-year mobile app maturity
   - Folk Care's mobile EVV is work in progress
   - **Likelihood:** High (agencies require mobile EVV for federal mandate compliance)
   - **Mitigation:** Prioritize mobile app completion (table stakes for market entry)

5. **Claims Processing Gap:**
   - Agencies with >100 patients need automated claims submission (Axxess strength)
   - Folk Care's basic invoicing insufficient for large agencies
   - **Likelihood:** High for medium/large agencies
   - **Mitigation:** Target small agencies (1-50 patients) where manual invoicing is acceptable, add automated claims long-term
